Terms of Use

Terms for using the RailSNTGyan technical documentation and reference portal.

Effective / Last updated: 26 October 2026
In brief: Use this portal only with permission, respect document confidentiality and confirm technical requirements against current official sources.

01. Use of the portal

RailSNTGyan provides a document/reference portal relating to Indian Railway S&T topics, approvals and learning materials. Access to some features is restricted to registered and authorized users. The portal is a supporting resource and does not replace current official manuals, instructions or sanction orders.

02. Authorized access

Users must use their own authorized accounts and safeguard their credentials. Users may not try to access records or functions for which they do not have permission, or share account credentials with others.

03. Documents and uploads

Users who upload, link or share documents must have the right and authorization to do so. They are responsible for accuracy, confidentiality, copyright and appropriate Google Drive sharing permissions. Uploaded files may be stored within the connected Google Drive account and indexed by link and record metadata in the portal.

04. Accuracy and availability

Reference links, document availability and portal functionality may change. Documents must be verified against the latest applicable official instructions before being relied on for operational or safety-related decisions. The portal is offered as available; uninterrupted access is not guaranteed.

05. Privacy and third parties

Use of the portal is also subject to the Privacy Policy. Google Drive, Firebase and external links may have additional provider policies or access requirements.

06. Contact

Questions about portal access, documents or these terms may be sent to systemsignalling@gmail.com.
