Privacy Policy

How RailSNTGyan handles account information, document records and Google Drive access.

Effective / Last updated: 26 October 2026
In brief: The portal uses Firebase to sign in users and store document records. With your permission, the Google Drive upload tool stores selected files in the connected Drive account.

01. Overview

RailSNTGyan is a Railway Signal & Telecommunication knowledge and document-management portal. It provides access to technical references, document records, approvals and learning materials for authorized users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the portal and its Google Drive upload feature.

02. Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: email address, Firebase Authentication user ID, sign-in status, account verification and applicable access roles or profile details.
  • Documents and record details: information entered in forms such as section/category, station or section, division, financial year, dates, document title, and Google Drive file links.
  • Uploaded files: documents that an authorized administrator selects for Google Drive upload, including filenames and file content sent to the selected Google account.
  • Limited browser/session data: sign-in session and interface settings needed for the portal to operate. The website uses browser session storage for some settings.

03. How information is used

We use this information to authenticate registered users, apply access permissions, display and manage portal records, upload requested files, organize them in appropriate Google Drive folders, open documents, maintain data accuracy, and respond to security or support requests.

04. Google Drive authorization and access

When an authorized administrator chooses Upload to Drive, RailSNTGyan requests Google authorization using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. This allows the app to create or manage files and folders it creates or that are explicitly made available to it. With your authorization, the app creates relevant section, subsection and financial-year folders, sends the selected file directly to Google Drive, and retrieves the file ID for a link in the portal record. This permission is not a request to read your entire Google Drive, Gmail, or contacts.

Google OAuth access tokens are used in the active browser session for Drive requests. The examined upload implementation does not save these Google access tokens into Firestore. The Google account holder may revoke permission at any time from Google Account security settings.

05. Document links and sharing

RailSNTGyan stores or displays Google Drive file links entered manually or created following an upload. A link alone does not override Google Drive permissions: whether a recipient can open a file depends on that file’s sharing settings. Administrators should review sharing permissions before distributing documents or granting users access. Removing a database record does not automatically delete the corresponding file from Google Drive, and deleting a Drive file may leave an outdated link in the portal.

06. Service providers and third-party services

The portal is served through GitHub Pages and uses Google Firebase services for account authentication and record/profile storage. The optional upload feature communicates with Google Identity Services and Google Drive. When you open external document links or use Google’s services, their own privacy policies and controls also apply. We do not sell users’ personal information or use Google Drive data to target advertising.

07. Retention and deletion

Portal account/profile details and document records may remain until they are corrected or removed by an authorized administrator or as required for legitimate recordkeeping. Google Drive documents remain under the applicable Drive account’s storage and retention controls. If you request correction or deletion, we will review the request and any applicable access, legal or recordkeeping requirements. Removing access to the portal does not itself remove documents already stored in Google Drive.

08. Security and access

The portal uses Firebase Authentication and role-based controls for features restricted to administrators or registered users. Google Drive authorization is handled by Google’s OAuth service. Access to a saved document still depends on its Drive sharing permissions. No internet service can guarantee perfect security. Users should protect their credentials and avoid uploading sensitive documents unless authorized.

09. Your choices and requests

You may choose whether to authorize Google Drive access. You can revoke the app’s access in your Google Account, or contact the portal administrator about your account, a document link, corrections or a deletion request. Send privacy requests to systemsignalling@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords or access tokens.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when portal functionality, service providers or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect material revisions. Changes to Google user-data use should be disclosed before data is used for any new purpose.
