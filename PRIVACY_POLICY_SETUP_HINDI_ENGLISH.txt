GITHUB PAGES UPLOAD
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Keep your current folders and images as they are.
3. Upload index.html, privacy.html and terms.html to the ROOT of your GitHub Pages repository (same level as existing index.html).
4. Wait for GitHub Pages deployment; open in an incognito tab:
    https://railsntgyan.github.io/privacy.html
    https://railsntgyan.github.io/terms.html
5. Confirm both links open WITHOUT sign-in and that the login footer has a Privacy Policy link.
6. In Google Cloud > Google Auth Platform > Branding enter:
    Application home page: https://railsntgyan.github.io/
    Privacy policy:       https://railsntgyan.github.io/privacy.html
    Terms of service:     https://railsntgyan.github.io/terms.html
7. Save changes and check Google Auth Platform > Audience for publishing status. Production publication/verification may require further steps, including proof of website domain ownership.

SECURITY NOTE
Do not upload OAuth client secrets or Google access tokens to public GitHub. A Web OAuth Client ID in frontend HTML is public by design.
